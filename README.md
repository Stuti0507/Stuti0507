### 🚀 Projects
 
**Web Development**
 
🔹 **[RESQ](https://github.com/Stuti0507/RESQ)** — [Live demo](https://resq-omega.vercel.app)<br>
Location-aware safety & emergency-response app built with React, Vite and live maps (Leaflet + Google Maps). *"Seconds matter. ResQ makes every one count."*
 
🔹 **[Emma](https://github.com/Stuti0507/Emma)** — [Live demo](https://emma-psi.vercel.app)<br>
A 24/7 AI companion for emotional support, built at a hackathon with a 3-person team (*The Codophilics*). My role: research, frontend development & graphic design.
 
🔹 **[MediKnow](https://github.com/Stuti0507/MediKnow)** — [Live demo](https://medi-know.vercel.app)<br>
A medicine-information app built so every prescription is a little easier to understand.
 
🔹 **[PLANOVA](https://github.com/Stuti0507/PLANOVA)** — [Live demo](https://planova-theta.vercel.app)<br>
A task manager & productivity tracker with progress stats and motivational nudges to keep your day on track.
 
**AI / ML & Data Analysis**
 
🔸 **[EcoTorque](https://github.com/Stuti0507/EcoTorque)**<br>
AI-powered EV comparison platform (Python, Streamlit, Gemini 2.5 Flash) that scores vehicles with a composite "Innovation Score" beyond just range and price.
 
🔸 **[EV Price & Range Predictor](https://github.com/Stuti0507/EV_PROJECT_ML)**<br>
ML-driven Streamlit app predicting electric-vehicle price and range from real-world population data.
 
🔸 **[Netflix Content Analysis](https://github.com/Stuti0507/VOIS_AICTE_Oct2025_MajorProject_STUTI_SHUKLA)**<br>
Exploratory data analysis on Netflix's catalog — content trends, ratings and country-wise distribution — built during my AICTE–VOIS AI/ML internship.
 
🔸 **[FareFlux](https://github.com/Stuti0507/FareFlux)**<br>
A 7-page Power BI dashboard turning 148,767 taxi trip records into insights on revenue, cancellations and location performance.
